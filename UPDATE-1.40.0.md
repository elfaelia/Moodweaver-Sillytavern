# Moodweaver 1.40.0: combos, cravings, more Bonds and real strength

Install the new `meters.js` and `reactive.js` too.

## Combos
Two things at once fire a bigger effect, shown as a ⚡ banner on the Turn-ons tab and as a COMBO line in the note.
- **Built in:**
  - **Claim:** defiance lit while possessive.
  - **Undone:** tears lit while guilty or soft.
  - **Feeding on it:** fear or tears lit while sadistic.
  - **Mine:** anything lit while very jealous.
  - **Hate-heat:** aching desire while angry.
  - **Good:** obedience lit while fond.
  - **Rush:** danger lit while horny.
  - **Keeping:** vulnerability lit while possessively tender.
  - **Snap:** a hot button pressed while frayed.
  - **Wounded:** a want blocked while insecure.
  - **Breaking point:** a need low and a temper going.
  - **Into the obsession:** anything lit with Obsession or Fascination high.
  - **Shame spiral:** desire with shame high.
  - **Craving met:** see below.
- **Your own combos:** Turn-ons tab → Combos & cravings. Pick two things (a turn-on lighting up, a feeling at clear or above, a bedroom trait lighting up, a hot button, a low need), name it and say what happens.
- At most two combos fire at once.

## Cravings
- A strong turn-on that hasn't lit for 8 replies becomes a craving (5 replies for intense and above). Claude is told the character starts steering toward it with hints, tests and small provocations.
- When a craving is finally lit, **Craving met** fires.
- There's no tolerance, as asked.
- Cravings and combos each have a switch, and both are worked out from the replies still in the chat, so swipes undo them.

## Strength, reworked
**Before:** most feelings sent the same words at every strength, and the note grouped them by category.

**Now:**
- **Strongest first:** feelings come first, in one list, strongest first, so priority is obvious.
- **Every line says how much it shows.** At faint, subtle and strong and above, the line itself says how much the feeling shows, e.g. “(takes over their voice and the main action)”. Claude no longer has to look the strength word up at the top. Mild and clear stay short.
- **Strong relationship lines** carry the same reminder.
- **Renamed feelings stay:** the ~30 that are renamed as they grow (irritated → angry → furious) still are.
