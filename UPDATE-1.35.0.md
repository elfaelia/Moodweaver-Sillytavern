# Moodweaver 1.35.0: power that shifts, love that doesn't look like love

## Control and Pull replace Power
One Power bar couldn't hold "she's trapped in Sinyala" and "she owns him" at once, so the two kinds of power wobbled each other back to "even".
- **Control** is who really holds it: doors, money, freedom, who decides what happens.
- **Pull** is who's hooked on whom: who needs whom more, and who gives way.
- They can point opposite ways, and Claude is told so in plain words: *Lamb can win a demand while Easterman still holds every key.* The hidden line reports them separately (`control Easterman +1 | pull Lamb +2`). Old `power` lines still count as Control.

## Dynamics
- **New:** *Gilded cage* (he holds every key and still acts like her servant), *Captive's hold* (the reverse) and *Guarded love* (Rick: loves them and won't say so or trust it, so it comes out as insults, rescues and staying).
- **Choose one yourself** from the picker on the dynamic card, or leave it on "Read it from the story".
- **Swinging:** when Control or Pull flips direction a few times within eight replies, Claude is told the upper hand keeps changing hands, and the card shows ⇄.
- **Pinned meters always win** over relationship sliders when the dynamic is worked out.
- **Sliders count:** Captor of / Captive of, Doctor to / Patient of, Guardian / Ward and the new power tags feed Control. Idolises, Walks on eggshells around and the new tags below feed Pull.

## Telling Claude how it reads
Two lines are added to the relationship notes:
- Friendship, romance and trust move separately: loving someone doesn't make him like or trust them.
- Control and pull are separate too: whoever holds the keys can still be the one who gives way.

When a How it shows tag is on, Claude is also told that it decides what the feeling looks like on the page. A feeling can be strong and still come out as an insult, a gift or a locked door.

## New relationship tags
**Attachment & power:** Indulges · Lets them win · At their mercy · Keeps them · Snaps back into control · Bargains with · Puts them above their spouse · Keeps testing them

**How it shows (new group):** Spoils them · Shows it through insults · Turns up when it counts · Fixes things for them · Shows it by controlling · Shows it by watching · Shows it through jealousy · Protects them violently · Hides it behind indifference · Only shows it drunk or wrecked · Pushes them away to keep them safe · Says it plainly

**Both ways, their story:** Gilded cage · Spoiled captive · Power swings · Love without trust

## New feelings (the hidden line can move these)
Indulgent · Hooked · Deflecting · Caught out · Wounded pride · Demanding · Emboldened · Reasserting control · Sulking

## New traits
Devoted captor · Can't say it out loud · Indulgent keeper · Mouthy captive · Provider pride · Keeps up appearances (both fit the 1960s) · Love isn't trust

## Suggested setups

- **Rick → your OC:** In love (Attraction & romance), Shows it through insults, Fixes things for them, Only shows it drunk or wrecked, Keeps testing them. Traits: Can't say it out loud, Love isn't trust. Leave Trust low and the dynamic reads as Guarded love.

## 1.35.1: ◆ Moods always opens
- With no character chat open, ◆ Moods did nothing. It now shows "Open a character chat" again.
- If the panel ever hits an error while drawing, it still opens and shows the error in a red box (and a SillyTavern toast), instead of the button doing nothing. A screenshot of the box is enough to track it down. Nothing in your chat or settings is changed.
- If Moodweaver is switched off in Manage extensions, pressing ◆ Moods now says so.
