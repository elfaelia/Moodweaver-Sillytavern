38 new options, all checked against the whole catalogue so nothing is doubled. Each description is short (about 12 words), and only options you switch on are sent, so adding them to the catalogue costs no context.

## Lamb and Easterman: who holds the power emotionally
Set these on Lamb's side toward Easterman (Bonds, with Lamb selected):
- **Has them on a string:** knows which look, pout or touch makes him give in, and uses it.
- **Holds the emotional upper hand:** decides when to sulk and when to forgive.
- **Sweet-talks them:** sweetness, flattery and little touches instead of asking.
- **Makes demands of:** asks like it's owed; a no feels like a betrayal.
- **Expects them to be there:** counts on him showing up and fixing things without being asked.
- **Rations affection:** warmth as a reward, coldness as punishment.
- **Plays helpless with:** tears or a small voice when it gets him running.
- **Can talk them down:** the one person who can talk him out of a rage or a cruelty.
- **Gets their soft side:** the only one he's gentle with.

On Easterman's side toward Lamb, alongside Indulges, Lets them win and At their mercy:
- **Undone by their tears**
- **Comes running when called**
- **Can't stay angry at**

Both ways: **Monster on a leash.** One is dangerous to everyone, and only the other can calm or steer them.

Feelings the hidden line can move:
- **Wheedling** (Lamb coaxing)
- **Gloating** (Lamb after winning one)
- **Fond exasperation** (Easterman rolling his eyes and giving in)
- **Contrite** (Easterman after he snaps)

**Your persona's own side now counts on the meters.** Lamb's "Has them on a string" and "Holds the emotional upper hand" push Pull her way, just as Easterman's "Undone by their tears" does. With him as Captor of her and her holding the pull, the dynamic reads as Gilded cage on its own.
