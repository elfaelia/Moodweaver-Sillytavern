# Author inspiration

These are short editorial interpretations for mixing prose influences, not exhaustive descriptions of an author's work. Each active author sends one line containing their name and the same cue shown in the UI. Inactive authors send nothing. A single shared instruction keeps the current characters, scene and point of view, and asks for original wording.

The usual strength bands apply: a faint influence adds occasional touches; 100 is Maximum and carries the prose throughout the reply. Several authors can be blended. This is prompting guidance, not a promise of exact imitation by any particular model.

The added writing techniques cover voice, sentence rhythm, implied meaning and narrative form. They are separate from genre. Le Guin's [discussion of rhythm and story](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/bvc-navigating-the-ocean-of-story-session-2-round-1) informed this separation. Short concrete cues follow [Anthropic's advice on clear, explicit prompting](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices).
