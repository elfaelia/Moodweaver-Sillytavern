# Moodweaver 1.16.0

100 new selectable entries: ten each in Psychosexual, Kinks, Roles & dynamics and Cruel & controlling; eight in Dark story pressure; twenty moodlets across the other categories; sixteen individual personality traits; and sixteen shared relationship settings.

Everything starts at zero. Existing selections, descriptions, strength rules and persona knowledge tiers are unchanged. The MBTI picker is unchanged; these are individual traits in the regular Personality category. The mobile search layout fix from 1.15.1 is retained.

## Prompt size and strength

Each new character entry sends one short description with its existing strength word. Story and relationship entries also explain what the setting means. No new instruction block or repeated scale is added. These use the existing character, persona and cast routes, so persona knowledge still controls what another character can know.

Unselected entries add nothing to the roleplay prompt. The optional dynamic analyser still receives catalogue information, as before, so a larger catalogue does add a little to those separate analysis requests.

The descriptions name a tendency without baking in maximum intensity. The shared strength scale supplies its weight; enabling one doesn't turn others off or average them away. Some settings describe a structure rather than an amount: strength makes an open relationship, a role or a shared history more prominent, not more factually true. Results still depend on the model and the rest of the prompt.

Search works through the existing catalogue and saved-list controls. Several niche terms also have aliases, including bootblacking, pony play, dom bottom, sub top, rope art, NRE and both spellings of impostor.

## Added entries

### Psychosexual

- **Responsive desire:** desire builds in response to welcome closeness and atmosphere.
- **Spontaneous desire:** desire arrives on its own, before anything has happened between them.
- **Novelty-led desire:** newness and discovery feed their attraction; familiarity changes its pull.
- **Desire through distance:** being apart gives their imagination room and makes reunion feel charged.
- **Drawn to being desired:** feeling wanted draws them in and makes their own interest grow.
- **Desire as validation:** being desired feels like proof of their worth; indifference stings personally.
- **Performance anxiety:** worries about measuring up during intimacy and gets stuck judging themselves.
- **Fantasy–reality gap:** likes an idea in fantasy without necessarily wanting it to happen.
- **Compersion:** takes pleasure in a partner’s happiness with others, alongside any mixed feelings.
- **Overanalysing attraction:** keeps picking apart what their attraction means instead of simply feeling it.

### Kinks

- **Leather:** drawn to leather’s scent, texture and look as part of intimacy.
- **Latex:** drawn to the shine, close fit and feel of latex clothing.
- **Gloves:** drawn to gloved hands and the formality or mystery they suggest.
- **Boots:** drawn to boots, their shape, finish and the presence they give someone.
- **Uniforms:** drawn to uniforms and the authority or belonging they suggest.
- **Formalwear:** drawn to tailored suits, evening dress and carefully put-together appearances.
- **Mirror play:** likes mirrors making shared moments visible from another angle.
- **Decorative bondage:** drawn to rope as adornment, with the pattern and presentation part of its appeal.
- **Tickling:** enjoys playful tickling and the laughter and anticipation around it.
- **ASMR / quiet sounds:** drawn to whispers and small close-up sounds during intimate moments.

### Roles & dynamics

- **Bootblack:** enjoys careful boot and leather care as a personal ritual or service.
- **Pet handler:** takes the guiding role in pet play, with cues, routines and encouragement.
- **Pony (pet play):** plays a proud or spirited pony, with presentation, grooming and guided movement.
- **Disciplinarian:** takes a structured role built around agreed rules, correction and follow-through.
- **Task-focused submissive:** likes clear tasks and the satisfaction of completing them for a partner.
- **Dominant bottom:** prefers the receiving role while directing the scene and keeping authority.
- **Submissive top:** prefers the active role while following a partner’s lead.
- **Egalitarian dynamic:** prefers shared initiative and decisions, with neither person taking authority.
- **Deity (roleplay):** plays a revered figure, receiving ceremonial attention and symbolic offerings.
- **Devotee (roleplay):** plays the devoted counterpart, expressing reverence through ceremony and offerings.

### Cruel & controlling

- **Punitive:** treats mistakes and defiance as things that deserve punishment.
- **Malicious compliance:** follows the letter of a request to spite the person who made it.
- **Scapegoating:** loads blame onto someone convenient so their own faults stay out of view.
- **Weaponised kindness:** turns favours and apparent concern into debts people are expected to repay.
- **Sabotaging:** wants other people’s efforts to fail and quietly undermines them.
- **Divide and rule:** sets people against each other to keep themselves at the centre.
- **Petty tyrant:** uses small amounts of authority to make everyday life difficult for others.
- **Blackmailing:** uses someone’s secrets as leverage in threats and demands.
- **Public humiliation:** wants an audience for someone’s embarrassment and loss of standing.
- **Schadenfreude:** takes satisfaction in other people’s setbacks, even without causing them.

### Dark story pressure

- **Institutional betrayal:** institutions protect their own reputation while the people relying on them pay the price.
- **Conspiracy of silence:** several people know something is wrong and keep choosing silence.
- **False refuge:** a place or person offering safety turns out to be part of the danger.
- **Ordinary evil:** cruelty fits into ordinary routines, polite conversation and respectable lives.
- **Uncanny familiar:** small wrong details make familiar places and routines feel increasingly alien.
- **Complicity trap:** small compromises draw people into wrongdoing that gets harder to leave.
- **Unreliable evidence:** clues contradict each other, making each apparent answer open another doubt.
- **Social ruin:** secrets and accusations put reputations, friendships and livelihoods at risk.

### Happy & lively

- **Awe-struck:** caught by something extraordinary; ordinary concerns feel smaller beside it.
- **Celebratory:** wants to mark a good moment and make an occasion of it.

### Confident & driven

- **Vindicated:** feels proved right after being doubted and wants that recognised.
- **Rallying:** pulls themselves together after a setback and finds the will to try again.

### Love & closeness

- **Moved:** touched by what something means, with feeling catching them off guard.
- **Welcoming:** makes room for people and tries to help them feel included.

### Attachment & obsession

- **Secure in the bond:** feels steady in the relationship, with room for closeness and time apart.
- **New relationship energy:** a new connection feels absorbing; discoveries and ordinary firsts carry extra excitement.

### Sad & hurt

- **Homesick:** misses familiar places, routines and people who make somewhere feel like home.
- **Bittersweet:** pleasure and sadness sit together; the good part makes the loss felt too.

### Scared & uneasy

- **Dreading:** an approaching event hangs over them and colours what happens before it.
- **Out of place:** feels they don’t belong here and keeps noticing the difference.

### Hiding & showing

- **Playing dumb:** understands more than they let on and acts unaware on purpose.
- **Forced cheer:** tries to sound upbeat while strain keeps showing around the edges.

### Energy

- **Second wind:** gets a fresh burst of energy despite the tiredness underneath.
- **Socially drained:** company has worn them out; even enjoyable conversation now takes effort.

### Mind & psychology

- **Ambivalent:** wants conflicting things and keeps feeling the pull of both.
- **Cognitive dissonance:** what they believe clashes with what they do, and the mismatch bothers them.
- **Decision fatigue:** too many choices have worn them down; even small decisions feel like work.
- **Impostor feelings:** doubts they deserve their place and worries people will discover they fall short.

### Personality

- **Diplomatic:** looks for wording and compromises people can accept without losing face.
- **Open-minded:** gives unfamiliar ideas a hearing and can change their mind.
- **Dogmatic:** treats their beliefs as settled truth and dismisses challenges to them.
- **Literal-minded:** takes wording at face value and may miss hints or implied meanings.
- **Frugal:** careful with resources and reluctant to waste what could still be useful.
- **Spendthrift:** spends freely and lets immediate wants crowd out the cost.
- **Opportunistic:** spots an opening and changes plans when it offers an advantage.
- **Transactional:** keeps track of what people give and get, even in personal relationships.
- **Persuasive:** shapes their argument around what matters to the person listening.
- **Sore loser:** takes losing personally and struggles to give the winner credit.
- **Overexplainer:** adds reasons and qualifications until a simple point becomes a long explanation.
- **Gossipy:** collects other people’s news and can barely resist passing it on.
- **Pedantic:** gets caught on exact wording and small errors, even when the point is clear.
- **Adaptable:** changes approach when circumstances shift without needing everything to go to plan.
- **Resilient:** finds a way forward after setbacks while still carrying what they cost.
- **Principled:** sticks to their values even when doing so costs them something.

### Relationships → What they are

- **Reluctant allies:** working together because they need to, with distrust still in the way.
- **Estranged friends:** used to be close; old familiarity keeps meeting the distance between them.
- **Pen pals:** a connection built through letters or messages, with distance shaping what they share.
- **Long distance:** a relationship shaped by time apart, limited contact and anticipated reunions.
- **Queerplatonic partners:** a committed bond outside ordinary friendship and romance expectations.
- **Open relationship:** together with an agreement allowing other connections; follow their established terms.
- **Polycule members:** part of the same network of relationships; direct bonds follow established history.
- **In-laws:** connected through marriage, with family expectations shaping how they get along.

### Relationships → Their story

- **Right person, wrong time:** the connection fits but their current circumstances get in its way.
- **Fast chemistry, slow trust:** attraction comes easily while trust takes time and evidence.
- **Mutual blackmail:** each knows something damaging about the other, keeping both tied to uneasy terms.
- **Shared alibi:** they depend on the same version of events and both have something to lose.
- **Public rivals, private allies:** they help each other in private while keeping up a public rivalry.
- **Growing apart:** their lives and priorities are drifting apart despite what they once shared.
- **Rebuilding trust:** trust was damaged; what they do now slowly earns it back or loses it again.
- **Bound by a promise:** a promise between them keeps shaping choices as keeping it becomes harder.

## Reading and inspiration

These are original short writing cues, not copied definitions or a clinical taxonomy. Community terms vary. The reading supplied ideas; the remaining moodlets, traits and plot settings are editorial additions chosen against the existing catalogue.

- [Passion & Soul: Kink and BDSM Glossary](https://www.passionandsoul.com/blog/info/kink-glossary) informed bootblack, pony and handler roles, decorative bondage, compersion, service, ritual and relationship terminology.
- [Scarleteen: How to Understand, Identify and Make Choices About Desire](https://www.scarleteen.com/read/sex-sexuality/how-understand-identify-and-make-choices-about-desire) informed the distinction between attraction, fantasy, context and wanting something to happen.
- [Planned Parenthood: Arousal](https://www.plannedparenthood.org/learn/sex-pleasure-and-sexual-dysfunction/sex-and-pleasure/arousal) provided context on desire varying across people and situations.
- [r/RomanceBooks: Dark romance with a good plot](https://www.reddit.com/r/RomanceBooks/comments/p9dzzi/dark_romance_with_a_good_plot/) suggested right person/wrong time and fast chemistry/slow trust as alternatives to adding more versions of obsession.
- [BFI: 10 great urban wyrd films](https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-urban-wyrd-films) informed ordinary settings becoming uncanny and threatening. The new dark plot cues focus on atmosphere, complicity and social danger.

No tests, browser runs or model generations were performed, at the owner's request.
