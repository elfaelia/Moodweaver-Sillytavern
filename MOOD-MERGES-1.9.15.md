Intensity now covers the main ranges: low anger is irritation, high anger is fury; low fear is unease, high fear is terror, and the highest fear is panic. Only the phrase for the chosen strength is sent, together with the existing shared strength rules. No extra scale paragraph is added per mood. Merged labels stay searchable under their old names.

Some clusters combine closely related shades, not just synonyms: sadness includes disappointment and dejection; guilt includes regret; sentimentality includes nostalgia. The current scene supplies the cause. Grief, loneliness, betrayal, shame and guilt remain separate from each other. Fear and anxiety remain separate too. In love stays separate from Smitten, and Princess dominance, masking, jealousy and possessiveness are untouched.

Saved blends keep the highest strength in each merged group and any pin. Persona knowledge follows that strongest setting, with the more private choice winning equal-strength ties. Source notes, defaults and undo snapshots migrate as before. Earlier aliases point straight to the final slider, so Irked and Annoyed both become Angry, and Laidback and Relaxed both become Calm. Migration never adds strengths together or revives a setting you later switch off.
