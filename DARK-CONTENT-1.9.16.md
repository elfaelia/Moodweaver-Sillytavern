# Dark-content controls in 1.9.16

This update adds 75 settings without turning any of them on by default. Disabled settings still add no prompt text.

## Character controls

- **Bold & driven:** Unhesitating.
- **Anger, conflict & control:** Murderous, Remorseless, Sadistic glee, Dehumanising, Calculating cruelty, Terrorising, Coercive, and Doing what needs to be done.
- **Mind & psychology:** Malignant narcissism, Grandiose narcissist, High-functioning narcissist, Sadistic personality disorder, Homicidal fixation, Moral disengagement, and Compartmentalised violence.
- **Archetype:** Serial killer, Torturer, Kidnapper, Drugger, Hitman, Captor, and Vigilante killer.
- **Status & secrets:** Has killed before, will kill again; Has killed before, trying not to again; Body count; Hidden trophies; and Escaped justice.
- **Morals & views:** Judgemental misogyny and Judgemental misandry.

The psychology labels are fictional character controls, not clinical diagnoses. Their prompt text describes behaviour so the model has something concrete to portray.

## Fighting style

The new character section includes Gutter fighting, Martial arts, Combat pragmatism, Brutal brawl, Combat parkour, Krav Maga, Systema / Spetsnaz combatives, Melee weapons, Grappling, Stick fighting, Sudden combat, Improvised weapons, Knife fighting, Close-quarters combat, Dirty fighting, Precision striking, Overwhelming force, and Trained killer.

These describe what the character can do. They can mix freely with the separate story controls below.

## Combat direction

Realistic combat, Gritty combat, Gory combat, Lethal combat, Tactical combat, Desperate combat, Chaotic combat, Sudden violence, Lasting injuries, and Combat aftermath control how the story treats a fight.

## Dark story pressure

Murder plot, Serial killer hunt, Killer POV, Stalking horror, Abduction plot, Captivity horror, Torture horror, Home invasion, Rising body count, No one is safe, Psychological torment, Escalating violence, Bad ending, Villain wins, and Cover-up make dark events part of the plot instead of background flavour.

Extreme horror and Splatter horror were also added under Genre.

## Stronger existing controls

- **Yandere** now describes loving behaviour toward the fixation alongside stalking, abduction, and lethal violence against rivals or threats. Its strength phrases run from a watchful edge to lethal possession.
- **Horror** scales from gathering dread to a threat that follows through with serious harm or death.
- **Dead Dove** is treated as an emphasis tag. It tells the model that the other active dark warnings are literal and should not be softened, subverted, or converted into a rescue.
- **Offensive fiction** scales from abrasive writing to deliberately transgressive character voice and subject matter without a moralising detour.
- **Psychopathic, misogynist, misandrist, violent, ruthless, cruel, abusive, predatory, tranquil fury, affably evil, soft-spoken sadist, pragmatic villain, mask of sanity, axe-crazy, gore, and guro** now use behavioural wording rather than relying on the label alone.

At strong and maximum levels, active dark character settings tell the model to let severe choices happen when they fit the character and scene. Active dark story settings likewise affect events, stakes, and consequences. The final weaving instruction still keeps every enabled setting at its own strength, so adding a second trait does not dilute the first one.
