# Catalogue cleanup — 1.9.14

The catalogue now has 897 sliders in the same 41 categories.

## Princess dominance

Added to Roles & dynamics for either character, including the player's persona:

> gets their way with girly charm, playful bossiness and a princessy expectation of being indulged, without taking full control

It uses the existing strength scale and persona knowledge tiers. It describes a way of influencing someone, without automatically assigning the other person a submissive role or writing the player's reactions.

Merging keeps the highest existing strength and any pin. It doesn't add strengths together. Defaults and undo snapshots migrate too. Persona knowledge and its source come from the strongest old setting; equal strengths keep the more private knowledge choice. With all merged sliders off, a saved knowledge choice is kept for later. Each observing character's knowledge is handled separately.

The retired IDs are removed during migration, so they can't switch a slider back on after you change it. Other values stay as they were. For example, jealous and possessive, dominant and controlling, and stoic and emotionless remain separate.
