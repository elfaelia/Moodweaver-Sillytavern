# Moodweaver 1.30.0

- **Feelings:** Giddy (Happy), Self-pitying (Sad), Walking on eggshells (Scared). Giddy is its own option again instead of folding into Excited.
- **Temperament:** Feral.
- **Body:** Burned, Wet (rain, shower).
- **Bedroom:** Wet (aroused).
- **Relationships:** Takes advantage of, Walks on eggshells around (Attachment & power).
- Already there, now easier to find: **Teary / tearing up**, **Victim complex / sees self as victim**. Mocking was already there as **Mocking / taunting** (feeling) and **Mocks** (relationship).
- **Desperate** now changes with strength, from "getting desperate" up to begging, bargaining, lying and taking risks at maximum. Fill its "because" so Claude knows what they're desperate for.

## Strength and blending
- The strength scale describes what each level does on the page. Maximum used to "own the reply", so ten maximums contradicted each other. Now maximums run at full force together, fused.
- Mild is a light, steady tint on how things are said and noticed, so it always makes a small difference without becoming a checklist item.
- Feelings: equal feelings at the top fuse into one state instead of taking turns.
- When two or more things are at intense or higher, the closing line says they hit at once and change each other.
- **Have it plan the blend while thinking** (Settings → What is sent, on by default): Opus 5.5 always thinks before replying. When three or more settings are strong, or any is intense, one line asks it to use that thinking to work out how they combine and which actions carry several at once, and to keep the planning out of the reply. Turn it off to save a little on thinking tokens.

## Info board
- The State row is led by the strongest feelings; Physical State names wet, injuries, drunk, tired and hungry, and lists active body settings, the needs and what the scene did since.
- The board still reports rather than steers, and doesn't invent feelings or needs to fill a row.
- Needs now notice burns, blisters and scalds (Health) and soaked or wet clothes (Comfort).

Tests show the same failures as 1.28.0 and no new ones.

- Every catalogue option and relationship was rendered at 100 and checked for wording that would hold it back ("a little", "now and then", "slightly" and so on). None needed changing.
- Sixteen core emotions used to stop at their 81 wording, so Angry at 100 still just said "furious". Each now has its own maximum wording with what it looks like: happy, excited, proud, affectionate, clingy, hurt, ashamed, sad, afraid, anxious, vulnerable, embarrassed, angry, controlling, energised, tired.
- Relationship feelings at maximum now drive what that person does around the other, even when it costs them.
