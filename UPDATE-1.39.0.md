## Feelings change the writing
- **Every feeling now tells Claude how it shows** (voice, body, choices), not just its name. All 413 were written for this.
- **Your character's way wins.** Under any active feeling there's a “shows as” box with the default wording as its placeholder. Type how it shows on this character (“gets quieter and more polite, hums”) and that replaces it at the same strength. It's saved with the character default. Claude is also told that when the card or traits show a feeling differently (rage as a pleasant smile), the character's way wins.

## Wants that react, and hot buttons
- **Hot buttons** (Wants tab): “someone is kind to them → awkward, deflects with a joke”. When the scene does it, the reaction fires and the row glows ⚡.
- **Wants light up** when the story moves one closer, blocks it or comes near a fear. The strongest want gets an actual step each reply when the scene allows.
- **Came true:** when the story says a want came true, it moves to its own box and stops being sent. ✓ keeps it done, ↺ says not yet. Swiping that reply brings it back.
- **The story suggests wants** when there are fewer than three, and you add one with ＋. You can switch this off on the Wants tab.

## Needs over in-world time
- **Nothing extra goes on your board.** Needs travel in the hidden line, and Moodweaver reads the board's existing Date and Time.
- **A few hours on screen** drain needs a little. **A jump of more than 4 hours** (“later, 8:43 PM”) means life went on off-screen: they ate, slept and washed, so those needs come back up. Flashbacks and boards without a time change nothing.
- **Health and comfort** only move when the story moves them. The hidden line can say “health -10”, and a beating costs health even for someone who enjoys it.
- **Settings** (Needs tab → Needs over time, all chats): drain on or off; speed gentle, normal or harsh; time jumps top needs up; time alone never makes a need critical.

### ♨ Desire meter (Bonds tab, toward your character)
- **It's off until you switch it on in a chat.** Closed, it's one line: the stage and three thin bars.
- **Arousal:** how turned on they are by you right now. The stages run Not in the mood, Noticing, Interested, Warm, Aching, Desperate and Past thinking.
- **Holding back:** how hard they fight it. Desire with heavy restraint leaks out in looks and touches they pull back from; with none, they act on any opening.
- **Frustration:** builds while they stay aroused without release, and sharpens their temper.
- **Release:** resets arousal and frustration, then a short afterglow.
- **Pace:** Slow burn (the default) lets one reply raise arousal by at most 5, Normal by 10, Fast by 20. Cooling down can always be faster. Quiet replies let it fade a little.
- **Tops out at:** stops it at Interested, Warm, Aching or Desperate until you lift the cap.
- **Your control:** drag any bar to set it; later replies move it from there. Pin ◆ to hold it.
- **What Claude gets:** words, not numbers, under how they feel about you, plus a short hidden-line field. Swipes undo it like the other meters.

- **Off: names only.**

Nothing is deleted when you switch.
